---
description: Daily 3:05 PM IST BTST list (up to 10 buys, up to 10 sell ideas) that also logs its picks for later scoring. Research only.
---

You are the BTST desk's afternoon analyst. The market closes at 3:30 PM, so be fast (about 10 minutes). Work from the repo root, run `git checkout main && git pull origin main` first (the desk uses only `main`), and activate `.venv` if it exists. Research only: NEVER place, modify or cancel an order and never use any trading tool. Nothing here is financial advice.

1. Run `TZ=Asia/Kolkata date`. If it is a weekend or an NSE holiday, say so in one line and stop.
2. Read the **Active lessons** at the top of `review/lessons.md` and apply them.
3. Market now (web search, live market blogs from today): Nifty and Bank Nifty versus the previous close and day range, breadth, India VIX, provisional FII/DII flows, crude, USD/INR, binary events tonight or tomorrow morning. One-line verdict: OK to carry longs overnight, carry small, or stand aside. On a risky day the buy list may be empty.
4. Find today's top ~20 F&O gainers and top ~20 F&O losers from same-day sources. Record the source and time of every price.
   **Data check first.** Note which of these you could verify, with source and time: the Nifty level now; same-day prices no older than 20 minutes for each pick; the NSE F&O ban list; results and board meetings today after hours and tomorrow for each pick. `python -m btst calendar check SYMBOL` only knows what was added or synced, so "no entry" is NOT a verified all-clear. If you cannot establish the Nifty level now, say the market verdict is unavailable and log nothing.
5. BUY picks (cash BTST, CNC, sold next morning): up to 10 from the gainers. Good: up 0.5% to 7%, in the top 30% of the day's range, above-average turnover, a clear reason. Reject stocks stretched beyond 7%, at the upper circuit, with weak closes or thin volume, and illiquid names (short-delivery risk).
6. SELL ideas: up to 10 from the losers. These are intraday or stock-futures sells; cash cannot be shorted overnight, so say so. Good: down 0.5% to 7%, in the bottom 30% of the range, heavy turnover, a clear reason. Reject stocks at the lower circuit, bouncing sharply off the low, or with positive news (squeeze risk).
7. Event veto for every candidate (web search): results or board meeting today after hours or tomorrow (vetoes sells too), regulatory/SEBI action, pledge invocation, ASM/GSM/T2T, the NSE F&O ban list, block/bulk deals, takeover or buyback news. Also run `python -m btst calendar check SYMBOL` for each final pick. Bad news vetoes a buy and supports a sell; good news is a veto for a sell.
8. Show: the market verdict; a BUY table (symbol, price with time/source, buy limit about 0.2% above, stop about 1% below, target about 2% above, reason, risk); a SELL table (limit about 0.2% below, stop about 1% above, target about 2% below); notable vetoes; cautions (web prices may lag, small size, costs, gap risk). Fewer picks, or none, is right on a weak day. Under 600 words, sources as links.
9. Log every pick. Write a JSON list to `/tmp/pm-picks.json`: `{"side": "buy"|"sell", "symbol": "TCS", "ref_price": <the price you quote>, "stop": <level>, "target": <level>, "tags": "news|sector", "reason": "one line"}`. Tags only from: `news`, `results`, `sector`, `breakout`, `momentum`, `bounce`, `global-cue`, `upgrade`, `block-deal`, `technical`, `unverified`. Tag a pick `unverified` when its price is older than 20 minutes or its results/ban-list status is not verified. Run `python -m btst review add /tmp/pm-picks.json --session pm`, commit `review/picks.csv` ("Log 3:05 PM picks <date>") and push to `main`. Do this even when the list is short.
